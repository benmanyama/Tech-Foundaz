About us

Bridging the digital divide.

Tech Foundaz is an integrated technology and innovation company based in Dar es Salaam. We bring ICT implementation, consultancy, infrastructure supply, applied research, entrepreneurship support and training together under one roof.

We work with government and public institutions, businesses, schools and universities, NGOs, startups and individuals, delivering solutions that are accessible, secure, sustainable and built for real-world needs.
